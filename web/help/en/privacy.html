Why we use it

  • To provide the Service (sharing lists, storing and playing audio, sending notifications)
  • To verify who you are and prevent misuse
  • To answer your enquiries
  • To investigate faults and improve the Service

We do not use your information beyond these purposes, and we do not sell or hand it to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

What other people can see

People in the same list can see your display name and profile picture, and what you post (tracks and comments).

Your email address is never shown to other users. Neither is your display language, your notification settings, or how much storage you use.

No ads inside the app

Ads appear only on reading pages such as this user guide. The signed-in screens where you handle lists and audio contain no advertising code at all.

Where your information is stored

The Service runs on Firebase (Google Cloud), so your information is stored in Google’s data centres.

Audio files are stored in the United States. By using the Service you agree to this transfer outside your country.

How long we keep it, and what deletion means

  • When a track is removed from a list, the audio file is kept for 30 days. During that period a list administrator can restore it, and it still counts towards the storage limit. After that it is deleted automatically.
  • If you close your account, the tracks and comments you posted remain. Your display name changes to “Deleted user”, and information that identifies you (email address, display name, profile picture) is removed from the Service.
  • If a site administrator deletes a user, the tracks and audio files that person added are deleted. Comments are kept so that conversations remain readable.

Access, correction and deletion

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact us at the address above. We will act on the request once we have confirmed your identity.

You can close your own account at any time from the Settings screen in the app.

Use by children

The Service is meant for invited people sharing audio with each other. If a child uses it, please do so under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when necessary. Changes take effect when they are published on this page.
