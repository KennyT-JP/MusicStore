What this app does

Track Cabinet collects recordings made by each member, along with links to tracks found on video sites and elsewhere, into a single shared list. You can have as many lists as you need, and each list has its own members with their own permissions.

Getting started usually looks like this:

  1. Create an account and verify your email address (see Creating an account).
  2. Get into a list. There are two ways: request a list of your own, or have someone let you into an existing one.
  3. Inside the list, add tracks and comments, and play the audio right there.

You are never added to a list automatically. To join an existing list, either ask a member for its Share URL and request to join (see Requesting to join), or ask a list admin for a share link (see Share links). To start a new list, you need a site admin's approval (see Requesting a new list).

Worth knowing

There is no screen that lists every list on the site. Unless someone tells you about a list, you cannot find it. That is deliberate: it keeps list contents from being stumbled upon.

Down the left side of the screen (along the bottom on a phone) you will find Home, Notifications and Settings. Site admin appears only for site admins.

Creating an account

On the sign-in screen, tap New here? Create an account. There are two ways to sign up.

  • Email and password — fill in Email address, Password and Display name, then press Create account. The password needs At least 6 characters, and for the display name, You can change this later.
  • A Google account — press Continue with Google.

If you sign up with an email address and password, verifying that address is required. You will land on the Check your email screen and cannot go further until you open the link in the message we send you.

Once you open the link, this page moves on by itself.

You can open the link in another tab, or even on another device. The app notices and moves on by itself. If it does not, press I have verified, continue. If the message never arrives, press Resend verification email. The verification email arrives in whichever language the app is currently showing.

Signing up with Google skips this step, since Google has already verified the address.

Worth knowing

Even if someone has sent you a share link, you cannot choose to join or view the list until your email address is verified. Finish the verification first.

Signing in and resetting your password

The sign-in screen puts Email address and Password at the top, then an or divider, then Continue with Google below it. Sign in the same way you signed up. The eye icon at the end of the password field toggles between Show password and Hide password.

Once you sign in, you stay signed in. Next time you open the app, you land straight back where you were.

If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot your password? just below the password field. Enter your email address, press Reset password, and a reset link is sent to you.

We sent you a reset link. Please check your email.

Password resets are only for accounts created with an email address and password. If you signed up with Google you have no password here, so use Continue with Google instead.

Worth knowing

If you open a share URL or a share link while signed out, nothing about the list is shown — not even its name. You get the sign-in screen first, and once you are through, you are sent back to the URL you opened. So if a link seems to show nothing, sign in first.

If Continue with Google seems to do nothing, your browser has probably blocked the sign-in window. The app says so on screen; allow pop-ups for this site and try again.
