What this app does

Track Cabinet collects recordings made by each member, along with links to tracks found on video sites and elsewhere, into a single shared list. You can have as many lists as you need, and each list has its own members with their own permissions.

Getting started usually looks like this:

  1. Create an account and verify your email address (see Creating an account).
  2. Get into a list. There are two ways: request a list of your own, or have someone let you into an existing one.
  3. Inside the list, add tracks and comments, and play the audio right there.

You are never added to a list automatically. To join an existing list, either ask a member for its Share URL and request to join (see Requesting to join), or ask a list admin for a share link (see Share links). To start a new list, you need a site admin's approval (see Requesting a new list).

Worth knowing

There is no screen that lists every list on the site. Unless someone tells you about a list, you cannot find it. That is deliberate: it keeps list contents from being stumbled upon.

Down the left side of the screen (along the bottom on a phone) you will find Home, Notifications and Settings. Site admin appears only for site admins.

Creating an account

On the sign-in screen, tap New here? Create an account. There are two ways to sign up.

  • Email and password — fill in Email address, Password and Display name, then press Create account. The password needs At least 6 characters, and for the display name, You can change this later.
  • A Google account — press Continue with Google.

If you sign up with an email address and password, verifying that address is required. You will land on the Check your email screen and cannot go further until you open the link in the message we send you.

Once you open the link, this page moves on by itself.

You can open the link in another tab, or even on another device. The app notices and moves on by itself. If it does not, press I have verified, continue. If the message never arrives, press Resend verification email. The verification email arrives in whichever language the app is currently showing.

Signing up with Google skips this step, since Google has already verified the address.

Worth knowing

Even if someone has sent you a share link, you cannot choose to join or view the list until your email address is verified. Finish the verification first.

Signing in and resetting your password

The sign-in screen puts Email address and Password at the top, then an or divider, then Continue with Google below it. Sign in the same way you signed up. The eye icon at the end of the password field toggles between Show password and Hide password.

In the iOS app, Continue with Apple appears alongside Continue with Google. Press it to sign in with your Apple account. This button appears only in the iOS app — not in a web browser and not in the Android app. Signing in with Apple for the first time also creates your account, and, like Google, it needs no email verification because Apple has already verified the address.

Once you sign in, you stay signed in. Next time you open the app, you land straight back where you were.

If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot your password? just below the password field. Enter your email address, press Reset password, and a reset link is sent to you.

We sent you a reset link. Please check your email.

Password resets are only for accounts created with an email address and password. If you signed up with Google you have no password here, so use Continue with Google instead.

Worth knowing

If you open a share URL or a share link while signed out, nothing about the list is shown — not even its name. You get the sign-in screen first, and once you are through, you are sent back to the URL you opened. So if a link seems to show nothing, sign in first.

If Continue with Google seems to do nothing, your browser has probably blocked the sign-in window. The app says so on screen; allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

Home: the lists you belong to

Your lists shows every list you belong to. Each row gives the list name, your role in that list, and how many tracks it holds. List admins also see how much storage the list is using.

Before you belong to anything, you will see this:

You have not joined any list yet.
To start a new list, submit a request. To join an existing one, ask a member for its share URL.

This screen is also where Request a new list and My requests live. If you are a list admin or above, the menu on a list's row offers Copy link, which creates a share link on the spot (see Share links).

A row may be tagged Lists without an admin. That means the list's admin has left, and it stays that way until a site admin appoints a new one. You can still browse and add tracks as before.

Requesting a new list

You cannot create a list outright. You submit a request and a site admin approves it. Start from Request a new list on Home. Anyone can submit a request.

A request asks for four things:

  • List name — A name already in use is not allowed
  • Estimated number of tracks
  • Expected number of users
  • Purpose

Once you send it:

Request submitted.
Please wait for a site administrator to review and approve it.

When the request is approved, you become the list admin of that list. It appears on your Home screen, and you can start inviting members and watching its storage. Names already taken — or already requested by someone else — are refused, and the app tells you so.

Worth knowing

The list name is fixed when you request it. There is no screen for renaming a list afterwards, so choose the name carefully.

Checking your requests

My requests on Home shows the state of everything you have submitted — both new-list requests and, under Your join requests, requests to join existing lists. Each one reads Pending, Approved or Rejected. If you have not submitted anything, you see You have no requests yet.

Approved entries have an Open or Open list action that takes you straight there.

Worth knowing

You are not notified when a request is rejected. Nothing is being hidden — we simply do not send a message about it. So if a request seems to have gone quiet, check this screen. A rejected request says so plainly.

After a rejection you can submit again with Request again, and there is no limit on how many times you may try. Administrators are not asked to give a reason, so no reason is shown.

Inside a list

Opening a list shows its tracks. Each row carries the number, the date, the title, the artist and who added it. Tracks with no title or artist show their file name or URL instead.

Sorting, searching and deleted tracks

  • Sort by — order the list by No., Date or Added by.
  • Search — the field reads Search by title, artist, or file name. When nothing matches you get No items matched your search.
  • Show deleted items — turn deleted tracks on and off in the list.

A deleted track keeps its place in the numbering and shows only Deleted. Its title, artist, file name and URL are not shown.

Worth knowing

Numbers are never reassigned. Deleting a track does not renumber the ones after it — that number simply stays missing. A gap in the numbering is not a bug. If you turn Show deleted items off you will see the gaps without an explanation, so turn it back on when you want to know what happened.

Deleted tracks never appear in search results. Search is meant for finding what is currently in the list.

The Show deleted items setting lasts only while the screen is open. Reopen the list and it goes back to showing them.

Where you can go from here

The Add button at the bottom right adds a track (Super User and above). While the list is empty you get There are no items yet. along with a hint about how to add one. Each row has a Details action leading to that track's own screen.

The screen menu holds Leave this list, and it is there for every member regardless of role. List admins and above also get Manage members, Join requests and List settings.

People who have left, or who have closed their account, appear as Former member. The tracks and comments they added stay where they are.

Track details and comments

A track's own screen gathers the title, artist, Date, Added by and the file or URL, together with every comment on it.

For uploaded audio and video, this screen lets you open the file in another app or download it. For a track that is a URL, following the link takes you out to that site.

Comments

Under Comments, use Write a comment to post. You can Reply to anyone, and replies to replies nest as deeply as you like. Before anyone has posted you see There are no comments yet.

Posting requires Super User or above. Read Only members cannot comment or reply at all (see What each role can do). You can edit and delete your own comments; list admins and above can edit or delete any comment in their list. A deleted comment shows as Deleted.

Editing, deleting and restoring

Edit and Delete appear for whoever added the track, and for list admins and above. When you delete, the app tells you how many days the file will be kept. Deleted tracks offer Restore to list admins and above, along with a line reading List administrators can restore this until {date}. (see Storage and deletion).

List admins and above can also use Copy a link to this track to create a share link pointing at this one track (see Share links).

Adding and editing tracks

Add inside a list opens the add screen. Tabs at the top switch between File and URL. A track holds either one file or one URL — never both.

  • On the File tab, press Choose a file. Once chosen, the app shows the file name and size along with the list's remaining space, as in {remaining} left.
  • On the URL tab, type the address into the URL field.

The fields below — Date, Title (optional), Artist (optional) and Comment (optional) — are shared by both tabs, and switching tabs keeps whatever you have typed. The date starts as today and you can change it freely. It is a plain calendar date with no time and no time zone, so it reads the same from anywhere in the world as the person who entered it meant it.

Save stays disabled until the file or URL is there. What is missing is spelled out next to the button, as Please choose a file or Please enter a URL.

How uploading works

The file is uploaded first, and the track is only created once that finishes. Along the way you see Uploading… {percent}%, and Cancel upload stops it.

Upload failed. Please try again.

If the connection drops, the browser closes, or you cancel, no track is created and no number is used up. That keeps half-finished entries out of the list. There is no way to resume a partial upload, so start it again from the beginning. A failed save leaves the screen open, so nothing you typed is lost.

If the list has hit its storage limit, the upload never starts and you see this instead (see Storage and deletion):

Storage limit reached. Please contact the list admin.

Editing an existing track

Edit on the track's screen reopens the same form. The title, artist, date and comment can all be changed and saved.

You can also swap the file itself. Choose a new file while editing and save, and the audio is replaced. The number stays the same — only the contents change.

The old file after a swap

The file you replaced is not deleted right away; it is kept for a while (30 days by default) and counts toward your usage during that time. After the period passes it is removed automatically.

If someone else updates the same track while you are editing it, your save is stopped rather than allowed to overwrite theirs:

Someone else updated this item. Please reload the latest version.

Press Reload to pull in the current version, then make your changes again.

Playback

In the track list, the control at the left of each row plays the audio right there. Which buttons appear depends on what is happening:

Current stateButtons shown
StoppedPlay
PlayingPause and Stop
PausedPlay and Stop

Stop halts playback and rewinds to the start, so the next Play begins from the beginning. Pause holds your place, so the next Play picks up where you left off.

Pressing Play on a different track stops whatever was playing. Two tracks never play at once. When a track reaches the end, it returns to the stopped state.

Worth knowing

The play control only appears on files that can actually be played as audio. Tracks that are URLs — a video site, say — cannot be played here, so open them from the track's screen instead. File types are not restricted at upload, so images and documents can be stored too; they get no play control either.

If playback fails, the app says Could not play this. Please try again.

About downloading audio from your browser

Downloading audio from a web browser will end. This will happen when the Android app is released.

You can still play audio directly in your browser as before. Sheet music and other files can still be downloaded as before.

Saving tracks to a device and listening offline is available in the iOS and Android apps (a premium feature). In the app, press Save to this device on a track, or choose Save this list to this device from the list menu, and it plays without an internet connection. Everything you saved is listed under Downloaded, and you can remove it from the device whenever you like. Removing it does not delete the track or the list.

Audio saved on a device needs an internet connection at least once every 30 days. After 30 days without one, offline playback stops. The files stay on the device even then — connect once and you can listen again. Before it stops, the Downloaded screen shows how many days are left.

Share links

A share link is a URL that lets someone see a list or a track. List admins and above can create them.

If you are handing one out

There are three places to create a link, and all three produce the same single link.

  • Copy link in the menu on a list's row on Home — it goes straight to your clipboard and the app confirms with Link copied.
  • Create a share link on Manage members — the URL is shown on screen, and this is also where you revoke it.
  • Copy a link to this track on a track's screen — this link points at that one track and opens straight to it.

Every link comes with this reminder attached:

It never expires and can be used by any number of people, any number of times. Revoke it to stop it.
Worth knowing

Share links do not expire, and any number of people can use them any number of times. Once a link is out, anyone you sent it to can forward it onward and it keeps working. Revoking is the only way to stop it. Decide who gets a link with that in mind.

To revoke, use Revoke link on Manage members. Afterwards the link can no longer be used to join or to view — but anyone who already joined or is already viewing stays. Removing them is a separate step on the members screen (see Managing members).

If you have received one

Opening a share link brings up A link was shared with you and the note Choose one. You can change this later. There are two options:

OptionWhat happens
Become a list member You become a member. You can add tracks and comments. Your name appears in the member list and you are notified when tracks are added.
View without becoming a member You do not become a member. You can browse the tracks and listen to them. You will not appear in the member list, receive no notifications, and cannot post.
Worth knowing

Choosing Become a list member makes you a Super User — you can add tracks and comments immediately. The other option is there for people who only want to look.

Either way, you must be signed in with a verified email address. Nobody browses a list anonymously. If you are signed out, you get the sign-in screen first and are returned to this choice once you are done.

If you choose View without becoming a member and later change your mind, just open the same link again and this choice comes back.

When a link does not work, the wording tells you which problem it is, because what you should do next differs:

This link has been revoked. Please ask for a new one.
Link not found. Please check the URL.

Requesting to join

Even without a share link, a list's Share URL lets you ask to be let in. Any member of that list can give you the URL.

Opening the share URL for a list you do not belong to shows little more than the list name and a Request to join button.

The contents of this list stay hidden until your request is approved.

Press it and your request goes off:

Your request has been sent. Please wait for approval.

You do not get to pick your own role. When a list admin approves the request, they are asked to Choose the role to grant and decide between Super User and Read Only.

Worth knowing

A rejected request produces no notification. Check My requests and you will see it marked Rejected (see Checking your requests). You may submit again as often as you like.

A share URL (which lets you request to join) and a share link (which lets you in on the spot) are different things. Share links start with /s/ and open the A link was shared with you choice screen.

People who left a list, and people who were removed from one, can both request to join again. The app does not distinguish between the two.

Managing members

Manage members is available to list admins and above, from the list's menu.

Changing roles and removing people

The heading reads Members ({count}) and gives the current count. Each member's menu offers Change role: {role} to change their role, and Remove from list, which asks you to confirm:

This member will be removed from the list.

Their items and comments stay, but their name will be shown as "Former member". They can request to join again.

You cannot remove yourself from here. To leave a list yourself, use Leave this list in the list's own menu.

People viewing without joining

Viewing without joining lists everyone who opened a share link and chose View without becoming a member. If there is nobody, it reads No one yet.

Worth knowing

These people are not members. They do not count towards the member total, they never receive notifications about the list, and they cannot add tracks or comments. This list exists so you can still see who is looking.

The two URLs on this screen

  • Share URL — Anyone with this URL can request to join. They cannot see the contents until approved. It costs you an approval step, but you decide case by case who gets in.
  • Create a share link — whoever opens this one is in straight away, with no approval. Use Revoke link to stop it (see Share links).

Incoming requests are approved or rejected on the Join requests screen. With nothing waiting, it reads There are no pending requests.

List settings

List settings is available to list admins and above. It covers two things: storage and deleting the list.

Storage used

Storage used shows the amount used against the limit, as "used / limit". As the list fills up, a note is added:

Over 80% of the limit.
Over 90% of the limit. Ask a site administrator to raise it.
Files from deleted items are kept for a while, so deleting does not free space immediately.

Only a site admin can change the limit itself, so ask one when a list runs short (see Storage and deletion).

Deleting the list

Deleting this list also deletes all of its files and comments. This cannot be undone.

Delete list asks you to confirm once more, naming the list. There are no backups, so anything deleted is gone for good.

Worth knowing

There is no screen for renaming a list. A list keeps the name it was given when it was requested.

What each role can do

There are four roles: Site admin, List admin, Super User and Read Only. Each role includes everything the roles below it can do.

Worth knowing

Roles are set per list. The same person can be a Super User in one list and Read Only in another. Your role in a given list is shown on that list's row on Home, so check there when you are unsure.

Read Only really is read-only. Beyond not being able to add tracks, a Read Only member cannot post comments or replies at all. Actions you are not allowed to take are not shown as disabled buttons — they are not shown at all. If a button seems to be missing, check your role in that list.

Anyone who opened a share link and chose View without becoming a member has no role. They can browse and listen, but they cannot post anything and do not appear among the members (see Managing members).

Roles are changed by list admins and site admins. Neither a share link nor an approved join request ever makes someone a list admin.

Storage and deletion

The storage limit applies per list. It starts at 1GB, and a site admin can set a different limit for each list. There is no limit on the size of an individual file.

List admins and above can see how much is used, both on the list's row on Home and under Storage used in List settings. Passing 80% sends the list's admins a Storage passed 80% notification, and passing 90% sends Storage passed 90%.

Once the limit is reached, further uploads do not start. You get Storage limit reached. Please contact the list admin., and the list admin should ask a site admin to raise the limit.

When a track is deleted

Worth knowing

Deleting a track does not free its file right away. The file is kept for 30 days by default, and it keeps using storage the whole time. That is why deleting things in a nearly-full list does not immediately make room.

The delay exists so that mistakes can be undone. During that window, list admins and above can Restore the track. The track's screen shows exactly how long is left, as List administrators can restore this until {date}.

Once the window passes, the file is deleted for good and the storage is released. At that point there is no way back. A site admin can change how many days are allowed.

Deleting a track leaves its number in place. Numbers are never reassigned, so a deleted track leaves a permanent gap (see Inside a list).

If an upload is interrupted — the connection drops, or the browser closes — no track is created, and the orphaned file is cleaned up automatically, returning the space.

Deleting an entire list works differently: there is no grace period. Every file and comment goes at once, and none of it can be recovered (see List settings).

Notifications

Reach Notifications either from the navigation or from the bell at the top, which carries a badge with your unread count. Notifications are listed newest first, and tapping one takes you to whatever it is about. Mark all as read clears the badge. With nothing waiting, the screen reads No notifications.

Worth knowing

You are never notified about your own actions. Adding a track yourself does not send you a notification about it.

Rejections are never notified. Check My requests for the outcome instead (see Checking your requests).

Added tracks are the one case decided by membership rather than role, so Read Only members get those too. In the other direction, even a site admin hears nothing about tracks in lists they have not joined.

Choosing what you receive

Open Notifications under Settings. All seven types above can be switched individually, and All notifications turns everything off at once. Everything starts switched on, and each type carries a line describing exactly when it fires.

Settings and leaving

Settings covers the following:

  • Display name — the name others see. Change it whenever you like and press Save.
  • Language — Japanese or English. This guide opens in whichever you choose.
  • Notifications — as described under Notifications.
  • Sign out
  • Withdraw from the service

Premium and coupons

Settings has a Premium section. With Premium you can:

  • Create a list without a request. Without Premium you submit a request and wait for a site admin to approve it (see Requesting a new list); with Premium you create one on the spot from Create a list.
  • Use more storage.
  • In the app, save audio to your device and listen offline (see Playback).

To go Premium, enter a coupon code. Type the code you received into the Coupon code field and press Redeem coupon. Once it is accepted, the screen shows how long your Premium lasts: Premium is active until {date}. when it is active, or You are not on Premium at the moment. when it is not.

Worth knowing

When Premium expires, the audio and lists you saved are not removed. Without Premium, everything you have already saved stays as it is. You simply cannot create a new list without submitting a request.

If a code is refused, the wording depends on why. A typo is fixed by entering it again, but a disabled or expired coupon is one to raise with whoever gave it to you.

Audio saved on this device (app)

This section appears only in the iOS and Android apps. A web browser has nowhere to save audio, so it is not shown there. Audio saved on this device shows how much is currently stored on the device. When there is nothing, it reads No audio is saved on this device. Use View downloads to reach the list of saved tracks (see Playback).

  • Download over mobile data too — off at first. When this is off, downloads run only while you are connected to Wi-Fi. Turn it on to save over mobile data as well.
  • Remove everything from this device — clears all audio saved on the device at once. Your tracks, your lists and the files you uploaded are not deleted. You can still play them online, and download them again.

Withdrawing

Your items and comments stay in the lists after you leave. Your name will be shown as "Former member".
This cannot be undone.
Worth knowing

Withdrawing does not remove the tracks and comments you added. The account goes; the posts stay. Only your name changes, to Former member, and your real name is no longer shown. Comments are kept because a conversation stops making sense when pieces of it vanish.

If you only want out of one list, do not withdraw — use Leave this list in that list instead (see Inside a list).

If you are the only site admin, you cannot withdraw. You will see the message below; appoint someone else as a site admin first.

You are currently the only site admin. Please appoint another site admin first.

Site administration

Site admin appears only for site admins — for everyone else the navigation entry is simply absent. It has five parts.

List requests

List requests holds every pending request. Weigh up the Requested by, Tracks, Expected number of users and Purpose, then choose Approve or Reject. Approving makes the requester the list admin of the new list. With nothing waiting, the screen reads There are no pending requests.

Lists and storage

Lists and storage shows every list with its usage and limit. Change storage limit sets a list's limit in MB.

Lists without an admin collects lists whose admin has gone. Use Assign a list admin to appoint a replacement — the list stays in that state until you do.

Users

Users lists everyone, with a summary in the form {admins} of {total} users are site admins. Each row's menu offers:

  • Add user — create an account with an email address, display name and password. As the screen puts it, You choose the password. Ask them to change it once you have handed it over. Accounts created this way count as already email-verified.
  • Make site admin and Remove as administrator — the change applies after that person signs in again, and the app says so.
  • Add to a list — put someone into a list directly. You must pick the role via Add as {role}, choosing Super User or Read Only; you cannot make someone a list admin this way, and existing members are refused.
  • Disable, Re-enable and Delete — see below.
Disable versus delete

Disabling can be undone. Deleting cannot. When in doubt, disable.

Disable — the person can no longer sign in and is removed from the lists they belonged to, but their tracks, audio files and comments are kept. Re-enable restores their access, though it does not put them back into their lists — invite them again if they need to be there. Disabled accounts are tagged Disabled.

Delete — removes the account along with the tracks and audio files that person added. Their comments are kept, shown as Former member. There are no backups, so this cannot be undone.

You cannot disable or delete your own account here. To close your own account, use Withdraw from the service in Settings. Nor can the only remaining site admin be demoted or deleted.

Coupons

Coupons is where you issue and manage the coupons that grant members Premium. Create one from Issue a coupon. You decide:

  • Months granted — how long the coupon's Premium lasts.
  • Number of people — how many people can use it.
  • Coupon expiry — choose No expiry or Set an expiry.
  • The code can be Generate automatically or Specify the text.

Issued coupons are listed, and each row's menu offers Disable, Change the number of people and See who used it. When there are none, it reads There are no coupons yet.

Worth knowing

A memorable code you set with Specify the text is also easier for others to guess. Always set the number of people and an expiry.

Site settings

  • Default storage limit for new lists — Default 1024 (1GB). Change existing lists from "Lists and storage".
  • Days to keep deleted files — Default 30. List administrators can restore during this period, and the space stays used.

Share links never expire, so there is no expiry setting to configure (see Share links).
